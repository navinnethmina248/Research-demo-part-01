This lecture is on Deep Learning Foundations. Deep Learning is a subfield of Machine Learning that uses Neural Networks with many layers to model complex patterns in data.

A Neural Network is composed of an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer, and it is loosely inspired by the structure of the human brain. Training a neural network involves an algorithm called Backpropagation, which adjusts the weights of the network to reduce error.

Convolutional Neural Networks, or CNNs, are a specialized type of neural network designed for image data. They are widely used in Computer Vision tasks such as image recognition and object detection.

Recurrent Neural Networks, or RNNs, are designed for sequential data such as text or time series, and they are commonly used in Natural Language Processing.

Researchers such as Geoffrey Hinton, Yann LeCun, and Yoshua Bengio are often credited as pioneers of modern Deep Learning, and their work has shaped the field significantly since the 2010s.
